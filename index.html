Methodology & Regional Traffic Distribution (v1.2.2)

This dashboard estimates real-time Battlefield 6 server populations using independent cascading behavioral proportion curves. Each APAC region is modeled around working adult evening primetime (peaking at ~21:00 local time), ensuring Sydney peaks earliest (~17:30 HKT), followed by Japan (~20:00 HKT), and Hong Kong & Singapore peaking at 21:00 – 22:00 HKT.

Proportions are benchmarked against Newzoo global player demographics and calibrated with US West cross-play queue baseline floors.
