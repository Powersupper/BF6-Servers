Methodology & Regional Traffic Distribution

This dashboard estimates real-time Battlefield 6 server populations using empirical hourly proportion weights derived from historical telemetry datasets, calibrated with a robust US West cross-play baseline floor to reflect active cross-regional matchmaking queues during APAC evening hours.

The regional hierarchy combines data across 6 primary vectors: Hong Kong (Local), SEA (Singapore), Japan (Tokyo), OCE (Sydney), US West (Real / Virtual), and Other Regions (EU / East US / Others). RedSec conversion rates are tailored to reflect regional market profiles (~5.5% for North America vs. ~3.0%–3.5% for APAC/OCE hubs).
