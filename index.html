Methodology & Regional Traffic Distribution

This dashboard estimates real-time Battlefield 6 server populations for gaming hubs accessible from Hong Kong under a 200ms ping threshold. By taking live global telemetry synchronization from Steam AppID 2807960 via a secure serverless Cloudflare proxy, the application dynamically proportions total activity across international time zones.

Each region's player volume is modeled around its unique local peak evening hours, accounting for circadian rhythm curves, weekend surges, and public holidays or holiday-eve boosts. Additionally, network latency penalties gently scale back distant server populations to reflect realistic connection accessibility from the local region, while distinguishing estimated anti-cheat (RedSec) protective footprint ratios (~3%).
